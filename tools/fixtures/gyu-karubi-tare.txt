【牛カルビ　甘辛たれ漬け】
黒毛和牛カルビを甘辛いタレにつけて、すき焼き風焼き肉にしてみました！
下味なので、漬けた後に焼き肉のたれで食べてもおいしいです！

[Beef Ribs Marinated in Sweet and Spicy Sauce]
We marinated Japanese Black Wagyu beef ribs in a sweet and spicy sauce to create a sukiyaki-style yakiniku!
Since it's a pre-seasoning, it's also delicious when eaten with yakiniku sauce after marinating!

【レシピ】
☆タレ☆
濃口醤油　大さじ２
料理酒　大さじ２
ごま油 大さじ１
砂糖　大さじ１
おろし生姜　小さじ１
おろしにんにく　小さじ１
豆板醤　小さじ１

これに胡麻などを混ぜるのもありです！

[Recipe]
☆Sauce☆
Dark soy sauce: 2 tablespoons
Cooking sake: 2 tablespoons
Sesame oil: 1 tablespoon
Sugar: 1 tablespoon
Grated ginger: 1 teaspoon
Grated garlic: 1 teaspoon
Doubanjiang (chili bean paste): 1 teaspoon

You can also add sesame seeds to this!
