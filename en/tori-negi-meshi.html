Chicken and Green Onion Rice

The seasoning is quite simple. If you prefer a stronger flavour, add more grated ginger or garlic. You might also add 1–2 shakes of salt before cooking.

Adding or mixing in condiments such as perilla leaves and sesame seeds is also delicious.

Serves
2–3
Time
23 min low + 3–5 min rest

Figures come from Instagram. A dash means it has not been fetched yet.

Ingredients

Chicken thigh鶏もも肉
1 piece
Riceお米
2 cups (300g)
Water水
400ml
Soy sauce濃口醤油
2 tbsp
Grated gingerおろし生姜
1 tsp
Grated garlicおろしにんにく
1 tsp
Sesame oilごま油
1 tbsp
Tip of a leek長ネギの先の部分
to taste

Leek sauce

Leek長ネギ
1/2
Soy sauce濃口醤油
2 tbsp
Vinegar酢
2 tbsp
Sugar砂糖
2 tbsp

For a stronger sauce, add more grated garlic, ginger and sesame oil.

  1. 1

    Rinse 2 cups of rice, drain in a sieve and let the water run off.

  2. 2

    In a pot, combine the rice, 400ml water, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp grated garlic and 1 tbsp sesame oil, then stir lightly.

  3. 3

    Lay the chicken thigh on top of the rice and add the green tip of the leek.

  4. 4

    Cover and bring to a boil over medium heat, then turn the heat down to low.

    23 min on low

  5. 5

    Turn off the heat and let it steam with the lid on.

    rest 3–5 min

  6. 6

    While it rests, make the sauce: finely chop 1/2 leek and mix with 2 tbsp each of soy sauce, vinegar and sugar.

  7. 7

    Take out the chicken and cut it into bite-size pieces, remove the leek tip, fluff the rice, then serve with the chicken and the sauce.

Notes

  • Want it bolder? Increase the grated ginger and garlic.
  • 1–2 shakes of salt before cooking tightens the flavour.
  • Perilla leaves and toasted sesame work either on top or mixed in.
