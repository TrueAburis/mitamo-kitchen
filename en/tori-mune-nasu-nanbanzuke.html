Chicken Breast and Eggplant Nanban-zuke

Notes

  • If you prefer the vegetables to be tender and soft, add them to the sweet-and-sour sauce while it is still warm. If you prefer a crisp texture, wait until the sauce has cooled down before adding them.
  • As for the eggplant, the perfect frying point is when you can easily pierce it with a chopstick—that's the time to take it out!
