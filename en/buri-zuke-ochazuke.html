Marinated Yellowtail Ochazuke

I used the fatty belly to make a marinated yellowtail bowl, then poured dashi over it. It's recommended because it's light and delicious while still allowing you to fully enjoy the umami of the fat!

Notes

  • *When pouring the dashi stock over the fish, make sure it's just slightly warm. If you pour it over at a boiling point or when it's hot enough to produce steam, the fish will cook. Be careful with the temperature so the fish doesn't turn too white.
  • Allow the rice to cool slightly before serving.
