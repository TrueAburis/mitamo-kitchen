Chilled Somen in Chicken and Bonito Dashi

It's getting hot, so I made chilled somen noodles with a refreshing and gentle flavor! The seasoning is on the light side, so add a splash of koikuchi (regular) soy sauce if you want it stronger.

Notes

  • Since the dashi is made partly from chicken, people worry the fat will set as it chills. In my experience it doesn't, not much.
  • I cook the somen a little short of the time on the package. Adjust it to your liking.
  • Okra can be eaten raw. However, if it has grown large and become tough, it is easier to eat after boiling it until tender.
